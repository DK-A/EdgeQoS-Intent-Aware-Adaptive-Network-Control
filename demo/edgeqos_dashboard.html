JURY DEFENSE PLAYBOOK

Top 4 Technical Defense Answers

Q1: Why CAKE over FQ-CoDel?

FQ-CoDel cannot prevent inter-flow starvation: a single video flow can hog bandwidth over voice. CAKE combines Cobalt AQM with Deficit Round Robin (DRR+) across 4 hardware tins and per-host isolation, eliminating the starvation problem completely.

Q2: Why TinyML instead of Deep Learning (LSTM/Transformer)?

Residential gateways run on budget ARM SoCs (Cortex-A53/A72). A deep neural net requires 10-50ms per forward pass and consumes >100MB RAM. Our Decision Tree classifier executes in 0.741 µs (sub-microsecond) and requires only 14.2 MB RSS.

Q3: What if the ISP strips or ignores DSCP bits?

EdgeQoS shapes egress traffic to 90-95% of bottleneck capacity. Because the bottleneck buffer inside the ISP modem remains empty, no queue forms at the ISP. EdgeQoS schedules packets internally before transmission, rendering ISP DSCP handling irrelevant!

Q4: How do you mathematically prove 124 ms FIFO standing delay?

Delay = (Buffer Size / Bottleneck Rate) + Propagation RTT
= (256 KB * 8 * 1024 b/KB) / 20,000,000 bps + 20 ms
= 104.85 ms + 20 ms = 124.85 ms.
Our real kernel measured delay is 124.00 ms (0.85 ms error).
