# Archive: Version 1 Synthetic ML Evaluation

> [!NOTE]
> **Correction and Lesson:**  
> Version 1 (v1) of our classifier was evaluated on a synthetic generator with artificially separable class distributions and reported near-perfect accuracy (~99.9%). We treat that result as an artifact of generator separability rather than real-world accuracy.  
> 
> All v1 outputs are preserved here for historical transparency and provenance. They are superseded by **ml-v2**, which evaluates real network stacks, pre-registered session-level partitions, safety metrics (false-promotion rate, risk-coverage), and real-application traffic.
